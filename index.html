From embryo transfer

For IVF pregnancies, use the transfer date and embryo age supplied by the fertility clinic.

IVF dating formula: EDD is 261 days after a day-5 transfer or 263 days after a day-3 transfer. Confirm with the treating fertility team.

    05 / CLINICAL UTILITIES

    Additional calculators

    05

    Body mass index (BMI)

    Use adult BMI categories only as a screening aid. For pregnancy planning, pre-pregnancy BMI is usually the relevant baseline.

    Formula: weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². BMI is not a diagnosis; pregnancy weight should not be interpreted using adult BMI categories alone.

    PRIVACY & ACCESSIBILITY

    Designed to minimize data exposure

    • Calculations run in this browser; there is no patient database or account.
    • No patient name, phone number or medical record number is needed.
    • Calendar downloads and print/PDF are generated on your device.
    • Use keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators and reduced-motion settings.

    Medical disclaimer This educational tool does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. Dating, screening and antenatal plans must be confirmed by a qualified clinician using current local guidance. Seek urgent medical care for urgent symptoms.
