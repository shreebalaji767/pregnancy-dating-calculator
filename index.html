Pregnancy Dating Calculator

Estimate LMP, estimated due date (EDD), and gestational age from ultrasound dating or a known LMP. No account, database, or server required.

Calculate from ultrasound

Enter the scan date and gestational age reported on the ultrasound.

Example: 36 weeks 5 days = 36+5.

Calculate from LMP

Gestational age is elapsed calendar days from the first day of the last menstrual period to your chosen date.

Calculate from date and gestational age

Use when a report gives a reference date and gestational age, for example 37 weeks 6 days.
