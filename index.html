        07 / OBSTETRIC CLINICAL UTILITY

        Clinical quick tools

        07

        For trained clinical users and supervised education. Enter only the minimum information needed; these tools run locally in this browser and do not save or upload entries.

        Mean arterial pressure (MAP)

        Enter systolic and diastolic blood pressure in mmHg. Formula: (SBP + 2 × DBP) ÷ 3.

        MAP is a derived value, not a diagnosis or a substitute for interpreting the actual BP, symptoms, repeat measurements and clinical context. In pregnancy, BP ≥160 systolic or ≥110 diastolic requires urgent clinical assessment.

        APGAR score worksheet

        Select the observed score for each of the five components. Use at the clinically appropriate time points (commonly 1 and 5 minutes after birth) and follow neonatal resuscitation protocols.

        APGAR documents status; it does not predict an individual outcome and must never delay resuscitation or other urgent care.

        Bishop score worksheet

        For a trained clinician documenting a cervical examination. Select the examination findings; scoring conventions can vary by local protocol.

        The score is only one input to induction decisions. It does not determine management by itself; consider parity, gestation, indication, contraindications, maternal/fetal status and local guidance.

        Obstetric history (GTPAL)

        Enter the documented counts. G = total pregnancies including the current pregnancy; T = term births; P = preterm births; A = pregnancy losses/abortions as defined by local convention; L = living children.

        This is a transcription aid, not a consistency check or replacement for the full obstetric history. Multiple gestation affects birth and living-child counts; confirm definitions and history locally.

        Urgent obstetric warning Seek urgent maternity assessment for heavy bleeding, seizure, severe headache or visual changes, severe abdominal pain, chest pain or difficulty breathing, fever with serious illness, fluid leakage or labour concerns, or reduced/changed fetal movements. Do not use these calculators to decide whether to delay care.
